# 第1話「拾いもの」 ショットリスト（Seedance 用）

- 総クリップ数：**21**
- 想定合計尺：**約2分40秒**（本編用の抜粋／PVとしても成立する構成）
- 記法：`C-01` = クリップ番号。`[i2v]` = 要キーフレーム画像、`[t2v]` = 画像なしで生成可
- **共通末尾ブロック**（[prompt-guide.md](prompt-guide.md) 第7節）は全クリップの末尾に貼ること

---

## S1 ─ 空が裂ける（アマベ上空 / 夜明け前）

### C-01 `[t2v]` 5s
```
An extreme wide shot of a pre-dawn sky over a dark sea. The sky is hazy indigo.
A thin seam of pale cyan light slowly opens across the upper sky, like a shell parting.
Camera: static.
Nothing else moves. Absolute stillness.
```

### C-02 `[t2v]` 10s
```
Thousands of small glowing pale cyan motes drift down from the opened seam in the sky,
falling as slowly as snow over a dark sea.
Camera: very slow tilt down, from the sky to the black water.
The water below begins to catch the light in scattered points.
```

### C-03 `[t2v]` 5s
```
Low angle from a stone stairway of a seaside town, looking up between tiled rooftops
and hanging laundry. Glowing motes drift down between the buildings.
Camera: static. Only the laundry sways gently and the motes fall.
```

## S2 ─ 起床（商會 二階 / 夜明け）

### C-04 `[i2v]` 5s
> キーフレーム：眠っている灯の顔アップ
```
Akari: dark blue-black cropped hair with ONE long right side lock tied by leather cord, freckles,
scar at right eyebrow end, deep indigo eyes.
Close-up of her sleeping face on a thin futon. Pale cyan light slowly creeps across her cheek.
Her eyes snap open.
Camera: static.
```

### C-05 `[i2v]` 5s
> キーフレーム：瓶の吊るされた廊下
```
A narrow wooden corridor with hundreds of small glass bottles hanging from the ceiling beams,
each holding a soft steady glow. Akari runs away from the camera down the corridor,
seen from behind, the bottles swinging in her wake.
Camera: slow dolly in, following her.
```

### C-06 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Akari: [一貫性キー].
Close-up of her hands pulling the leather cord tight around her single long side lock,
brass goggles with a cracked lens resting on the windowsill beside her.
Camera: static, shallow depth of field.
```

## S3 ─ 出航（波止場 / 朝）

### C-07 `[i2v]` 10s
> キーフレーム：波止場の全景
```
Wide shot of a small stone harbor at dawn, rusted cranes, a battered wooden salvage boat
at the pier. Glowing motes still drifting down over the water. Gulls in the distance.
Camera: very slow pan right, from the town to the boat.
```

### C-08 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Ren: tall lean slouching man, ash-blond messy shoulder hair, bangs over RIGHT eye,
matte black-iron prosthetic RIGHT arm with brass gears, stubble, tool belt.
He strikes the boat's engine housing once with the side of his black prosthetic fist.
The engine coughs into life, a puff of smoke rising.
Camera: static, medium shot from the side.
```

### C-09 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Ren and Akari: [両者の一貫性キー].
Two figures seen from behind, standing in the small boat as it pulls away from the pier,
the town receding. Neither turns around.
Camera: slow dolly out, low over the water.
```

## S4 ─ 潜り（海中 / 午前）※ 本話の映像的な山

### C-10 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Surface of dark water seen from just below. A silhouette breaks through from above,
a burst of white bubbles expanding toward the camera.
Camera: static, looking up at the surface.
```

### C-11 `[i2v]` 10s
> キーフレーム：[01_akari.md](../characters/01_akari.md) 5-4 のプロンプトで生成した画
```
Akari: [一貫性キー], brass goggles over her eyes.
She swims slowly downward, one arm outstretched, hair and oversized coat billowing upward.
Hundreds of small glowing spheres drift in the dark indigo water around her like slow snow.
Camera: slow dolly out, retreating downward ahead of her.
```

### C-12 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Close-up of one glowing sphere suspended in dark water. Inside it, a faint blurred image
of a woman's face flickers once, then settles.
Camera: very slow orbit around the sphere.
Steady soft glow, no flicker on the sphere itself.
```

### C-13 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Akari: [一貫性キー].
Underwater close-up on her goggled face, her indigo eyes reflecting the drifting lights.
She blinks slowly. A single stream of bubbles escapes her lips.
Camera: static.
```

## S5 ─ 発見（海中 → 海面 / 午前）

### C-14 `[i2v]` 10s
```
Extreme wide underwater shot, silhouette-heavy. Far below in the deep, a pale human-shaped
silhouette hangs motionless in a soft column of light, surrounded by drifting glowing spheres.
Akari's small dark silhouette descends toward it from the top of the frame.
Camera: static, immense sense of depth and scale.
```

### C-15 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Mika: pale androgynous child, uneven white jaw-length bob, near-colorless pale gray eyes,
bluish-white skin, oversized khaki coat, bare feet.
Close-up underwater, the child's face motionless, eyes closed, white hair floating.
The eyes open — pale, colorless, looking straight at the camera.
Camera: extremely slow dolly in.
```

### C-16 `[i2v]` 5s
```
The surface of the sea from above, calm and dark. Two figures burst through the surface
in a spray of water — a girl gasping, holding a pale child against her chest.
Camera: static, high angle.
```

## S7 ─ 名前がない（商會 一階 / 夕）

### C-19 `[i2v]` 10s
```
Chitose: 43yo woman, black low bun with gray temples and wooden hairpin,
gold MONOCLE on RIGHT eye with chain, long cream apron with three mending patches.
She kneels in front of a seated child wrapped in a blanket, one hand held out palm-up,
waiting. The child does not move.
Camera: static, wide, both figures small in a cluttered lamplit workshop.
Warm lamp light from the left, deep shadows.
```

### C-20 `[i2v]` 5s
```
Close-up of Chitose's open weathered palm and, above it, the child's small pale hand
hovering without touching. Neither hand moves.
Camera: very slow dolly in.
```

## S8 ─ 軒先の灯（商會 軒先 / 夜）※ 引きのカット

### C-21 `[i2v]` 10s
```
Akari and Mika: [両者の一貫性キー].
Wide shot at night. Under the eaves of a small harbor shack, dozens of glass bottles
hang glowing softly. A girl and a pale barefoot child sit side by side on the wooden step,
seen from a low three-quarter angle behind them, both looking out at the black sea.
The child slowly turns its head toward her.
Camera: extremely slow dolly out, until the shack becomes one small light on a dark coast.
Steady soft glow, no flicker.
```

> このカット尻に、ミカの台詞「ぼく、名前がない」を**字幕またはボイスのみ**で乗せる。
> 口パクは生成させない（後ろ姿の構図で回避済み）。
